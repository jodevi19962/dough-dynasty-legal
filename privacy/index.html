Dough Dynasty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

This privacy policy describes how Dough Dynasty, a free offline mobile game published by Jodevi Games (on Google Play under the developer name ThuisSaldo), handles information.

Personal data

Dough Dynasty does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. This release has no accounts, analytics, crash-reporting, advertising SDK, or active in-app purchase provider.

The game does not ask for a name, email address, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, device identifier, photos, credentials, or payment details.

Data stored on the device

The app stores gameplay progress only on the device, in private application storage. This includes virtual currencies, stations, cities, managers, quests, settings, and timestamps used for offline earnings, daily rewards, and cooldowns. Language and reduced-motion preferences are stored locally as app preferences.

This information stays on the device. Dough Dynasty does not send it to Jodevi Games or to any third party.

Purchases and advertising

This release does not include advertisements or real-money in-app purchases. Dough Dynasty never stores payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until the player deletes it or uninstalls the app. You can delete gameplay progress and app preferences from the in-game settings. Uninstalling the app also removes app-local data according to the platform's behavior.

Children

Dough Dynasty is made for children and families. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat, and no account. The game does not collect, transmit, or share personal data from anyone, including children, and it uses no third-party SDK that does.

Links that leave the game (to this policy, the terms, and the support mailbox) are behind a parent check.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. If a future release begins to collect data, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
